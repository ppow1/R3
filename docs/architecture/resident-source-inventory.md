A directory result with only Grace would describe a projection of current bindings, not HR registration; it would not mean only one HR/personnel record exists. Conversely, 11 HR rows do not establish 11 active directory bindings.

Thus the earlier Grace-only result cannot be treated as a full roster or reproduced from the current source; this work did not establish its prior timestamp/schema/digest.

The safe disposition is to preserve all HR records, represent Evelyn and GM individually with provisional minimum dossiers that contain no confidential office or relationship facts, and require the authoritative directory snapshot plus HR crosswalk before declaring full roster coverage or activation readiness.
